Grant Access

The person must already have a Firebase Authentication account (create one in the Firebase Console → Authentication → Users, or ask them to use "Forgot password" on their first visit once an account exists). This just grants their existing account dashboard permissions — it does not create the account itself.

Note on removing access

There's no revoke button here yet — for now, remove someone's role via the Firebase Console (Authentication → user → Custom Claims) or disable their account entirely. A revoke flow is an easy follow-up if this becomes a regular need.
